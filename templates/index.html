Our Mission

Bengaluru wastes thousands of meals every day. We're changing that.

A significant proportion of food prepared in Bengaluru's restaurants, tech campuses and large-scale events goes to waste every day — not because of shortage, but because of a coordination gap between those with surplus and those who need it.

SharePlate Connect aims to close that gap with a lightweight platform that makes food donation as simple as filling out a short form. The platform is designed to handle matching and status tracking so both donors and NGOs have a clear picture of every donation. Notification features are planned for a future release.

Why SharePlate Connect?

Designed from the ground up for speed, simplicity and real-world NGO workflows.

⚡

Fast Coordination

No phone-tag. No spreadsheets. A donation is submitted, matched and confirmed in minutes — before food spoils.

📍

Location-Aware Matching

Our matcher considers service area and food type so NGOs only receive pickups they can actually handle — reducing no-shows.

📊

End-to-End Tracking

Donors can follow a donation from submission to confirmed pickup. NGOs get a clear action list. No one is left guessing.

🕐

Time-Sensitive Windows

Every donation includes an availability window. Automatic expiry handling is a planned capability to keep listings accurate without manual intervention.

🔒

Simple & Reliable

Built with Flask and SQLite — lightweight, runs anywhere and requires no complex infrastructure to deploy.

📱

Mobile Friendly

Designed responsively so donors and NGO staff can interact with the platform on any device, in the field or at the office.
